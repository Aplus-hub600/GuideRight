Scenario Title

Urban Instability & Infrastructure Awareness in a Complex Megacity

Location

Dhaka, Bangladesh

Context (High-Level)

Following a high-visibility political incident in mid-December, reporting indicates increased protest activity, localized disruptions, and heightened security posture across parts of Dhaka. Information is fragmented, uneven in quality, and varies significantly by source type and geographic coverage.

An interagency analytic cell is tasked with maintaining situational awareness to support near-term risk monitoring and contingency planning in a dense urban environment. The tasking assumes no direct U.S. operational involvement and focuses exclusively on understanding the information environment.

Core Analytic Question

What is the current risk posture across Dhaka, how confident are we in what we know, and where should analytic attention be focused over the next 48–72 hours?

Analytic Objectives

Assess relative risk posture across the city (not absolute event counts)

Identify infrastructure considerations that may affect access and movement (e.g., healthcare, transportation corridors)

Represent human activity as spatial patterns and intensity rather than discrete claims

Explicitly communicate confidence levels, data gaps, and uncertainty

Support analyst-in-the-loop reasoning without generating predictions or recommendations

Time Windows

Baseline: Approximately 12 months prior to the incident

Focused Window: ~30 days before and after the incident

Recent Window: Last 7–14 days, depending on data availability

What the Demo Emphasizes

City-level and sub-area scope awareness where data coverage is defensible

Infrastructure-first spatial context, reflecting stronger data reliability

Pattern-based interpretation of activity rather than point claims

Transparent indication of data sources, coverage, and uncertainty

Concise analytic summaries designed to support analyst workflows

What the Demo Explicitly Avoids

Claims of precise protest or riot counts

Hyper-local assertions without defensible sourcing

Predictive or prescriptive outputs

Feature-by-feature walkthroughs unrelated to the analytic question

Intended Use

This scenario is an analytic vignette, designed to evaluate how GuideRight supports reasoning under uncertainty and situational awareness in complex environments. It is reusable across regions and does not represent an operational plan, mission rehearsal, or targeting activity.
