Implement a premium “Mission Profile Setup” and role-aware adaptive response system for GuideRight.

Objective:
GuideRight should adapt the user experience based on mission role, experience level, mission context, time horizon, and priority analytic lenses before the main dashboard loads. This should make the system feel more operationally mature, intuitive, and relationship-aware while preserving analyst-in-the-loop decision support. Do not make it feel like a consumer chatbot or generic AI assistant.

Core concept:
Same data, different user needs. GuideRight should not change facts. It should change how information is prioritized, explained, summarized, and displayed based on who the user is and what they are trying to accomplish.

Role-based behavior:

Intelligence Analyst:
- Prioritize source traceability, confidence, competing explanations, gaps, and analytic rigor.
- Prefer detailed summaries with evidence and uncertainty.
- Avoid tactical recommendations.
- Default lens priority: Threat Activity, Information Environment, Infrastructure.

SOF Operator:
- Prioritize operational relevance, movement constraints, immediate changes, infrastructure access, and high-signal alerts.
- Prefer concise summaries.
- Avoid long analytic theory.
- Default lens priority: Threat Activity, Logistics, Human Activity.

Commander:
- Prioritize executive synthesis, decision implications, major risks, and information gaps.
- Prefer concise key judgments.
- Default lens priority: Threat Activity, Infrastructure, Political Activity.

Civil Affairs:
- Prioritize population impacts, infrastructure services, local concerns, community effects, and stability indicators.
- Prefer contextual summaries.
- Default lens priority: Human Activity, Infrastructure, Political Activity.

Psychological Operations:
- Prioritize narratives, sentiment changes, amplification patterns, information environment, and influence indicators.
- Default lens priority: Information Environment, Political Activity, Human Activity.

Diplomatic Security:
- Prioritize personnel security, facility risk, mobility, public unrest indicators, and proximity to critical infrastructure.
- Default lens priority: Threat Activity, Infrastructure, Logistics.

Planner:
- Prioritize scenario comparison, constraints, assumptions, options, dependencies, and timeline.
- Default lens priority: Infrastructure, Logistics, Threat Activity.

Security Cooperation:
- Prioritize partner capacity, stability indicators, infrastructure dependencies, and regional context.
- Default lens priority: Political Activity, Infrastructure, Human Activity.

Executive Decision Maker:
- Prioritize top-line risk, strategic implications, confidence, and concise recommended areas for attention.
- Default lens priority: Threat Activity, Political Activity, Infrastructure.

Experience-level behavior:

Junior:
- Provide more explanation and context.
- Define analytic terms when useful.
- Explain why confidence is high, moderate, or low.
- Suggest follow-up questions.
- Provide structured guidance.

Intermediate:
- Provide balanced context and concise explanation.
- Include assumptions and gaps.
- Suggest reasonable next analytic steps.

Senior:
- Be concise.
- Emphasize evidence, confidence, gaps, and implications.
- Avoid over-explaining.
- Provide direct summaries and allow drill-down.

Expert:
- Be very concise.
- Provide source-linked evidence, confidence, and uncertainty.
- Avoid definitions unless asked.
- Surface anomalies and changes quickly.

Mission context behavior:

Garrison:
- Emphasize monitoring, trend analysis, and routine awareness.

Training:
- Emphasize learning, evaluation, scenario replay, and explanation.

Planning:
- Emphasize constraints, dependencies, assumptions, and options.

Crisis Response:
- Emphasize recency, escalation indicators, infrastructure impact, and decision speed.

Competition:
- Emphasize long-term trends, influence, political activity, and strategic positioning.

Deployment:
- Emphasize operational relevance, movement, logistics, threat activity, and infrastructure.

Assessment:
- Emphasize evidence, confidence, validation, gaps, and after-action learning.

Temporal reasoning:
Use the mission profile to determine how much data should be used to generate answers.

Default temporal logic:
- Commander / Executive: prioritize last 24–72 hours unless user asks otherwise.
- SOF Operator: prioritize last 24–72 hours and route/mobility-relevant signals.
- Intelligence Analyst: prioritize last 7–30 days, with ability to compare baseline trends.
- Civil Affairs: prioritize last 30–180 days when population or infrastructure effects matter.
- PSYOP: prioritize last 7–30 days, plus narrative trend comparison.
- Diplomatic Security: prioritize last 24 hours to 14 days depending on crisis context.
- Planner: prioritize mission-defined window and compare constraints over time.

If user selects “Mission Adaptive,” the system should choose the time window based on Role + Mission Context + Query Type and display the selected window in the response.

Example:
“Using last 14 days based on Senior Intelligence Analyst / Crisis Response profile.”

Confidence and source behavior:
Adjust how confidence is presented, but do not manipulate truth.

For analysts:
- Show source diversity, corroboration, recency, and gaps.

For operators:
- Show confidence level and operational relevance.
- Keep explanations concise.

For commanders/executives:
- Show confidence level and key reason in one sentence.

For junior users:
- Explain what confidence means.

For senior/expert users:
- Provide confidence and evidence trail without extra teaching.
